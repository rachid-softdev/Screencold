# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **B2B sales professionals and SDRs** who need to research dozens of prospect websites daily and craft personalized outreach at scale
- **SEO freelancers and independent consultants** who pitch their services to leads and need quick, credible audit data
- **Digital agencies** (web dev, marketing, SEO) managing multiple client prospecting campaigns
- Desk-bound, CRM-adjacent workflow. Time-pressed, juggling 10–50+ prospects. The primary job: go from prospect URL to a personalized outreach email in seconds, not hours.

## Product Purpose

ScreenCold automates the discovery-to-outreach pipeline for B2B prospecting. It audits any website (screenshot + AI analysis) and generates a personalized sales email from that analysis — turning hours of manual research into a 30-second workflow. Success looks like a user who opens ScreenCold, pastes a URL, gets an audit, clicks "generate email," and sends — without ever opening a separate research tab.

## Brand Personality

**Précis · Confiant · Efficace** (Precise · Confident · Efficient)

- Voice: professional but not corporate, French-native, direct
- Tone: helpful and confident — the tool knows what it found and isn't apologetic
- Emotional goal: the user feels they've done their homework, even when they haven't opened a single browser tab

**Reference lanes**: Lemlist / Instantly / Apollo.io — purpose-built sales tools with clean UIs, not bloated CRMs. The French SaaS tone of Pennylane or Folks.

**Anti-references**: "Cold email bro" aesthetic (dark landing pages, aggressive CTAs, flashy badges, hype copy). Generic enterprise blue SaaS clones (Salesforce/Zendesk cookie-cutter). Over-designed dashboards where the actual data is harder to find.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work** — Results first, configuration second. The audit output (screenshots, scores, issues found) is the primary value, not the form that created it.
2. **Trust through transparency** — Surface what the AI actually found, with specific quotes from the page. Never deliver a black-box score without evidence.
3. **Progression, not overwhelm** — Guide the user URL → audit → email in a clear, linear flow. Don't dump every feature on the dashboard at once.
4. **French-native credibility** — Copy and conventions written for a French B2B audience, not translated from English. No franglais UI patterns.
5. **Speed as a feature** — Every interaction should feel instant or show clear progress. The tool's value is saving time; the UI must not waste it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 Level AA as baseline
- Reduced motion respected: animations enhance but never gate content
- Color not the sole differentiator for status/changes
- Screen reader support for key workflows (audit results, campaign tables)
- French-language a11y labels and aria attributes throughout
